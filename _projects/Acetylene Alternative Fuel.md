# Acetylene as an alternative fuel

## Study

I worked on this team study during my 2018–19 Bachelor’s in Mechanical Engineering. We compared reported acetylene and petrol fuel consumption in a small spark-ignition engine at four speeds.

## Result

The report’s test data show lower acetylene fuel consumption at each recorded speed, averaging about 9% lower in this experiment.

## Scope

This is an academic test, not a market-ready fuel system. The report notes detonation above 1800 rpm; a later engine study found performance and emissions varied with load, and NIOSH lists acetylene as a flammable gas ([engine study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.energy.2018.03.108) · [NIOSH profile](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/npg/npgd0008.html)).
