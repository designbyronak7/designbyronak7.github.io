# Tap box and busbar layout

I work on tap-box assemblies that house busbars and connect to cable-bus systems. I check how conductors enter the enclosure, how the busbars fit, and whether installers can reach the connections.

The diagram is a general concept. It does not show customer details, project ratings, or production dimensions.
