# Tap box and busbar layout

<p class="project-kicker">Power Bus Way · generalized professional example</p>

## Design problem

A tap box has to accept incoming conductors and route them to busbar connections without blocking access to the bolted joints.

## My role

I check conductor entry and routing, busbar and support fit, enclosure clearances, and reach to the bolted connections.

**Design decision:** I lay out the conductor path and joint access first, then coordinate the enclosure entry and supports around them.

**Constraints:** available enclosure space and connection locations shape the layout. Customer dimensions, ratings, and geometry are not shown.

**Outcome:** I update CAD and drawings and help answer fabrication and assembly questions. The images below are references and concepts, not customer deliverables.
