## Design challenge

Coordinate the cable-bus route, enclosure interfaces, and support locations while maintaining clearance for cable entry, mounting, installation, and future service access.

## My role

As a Mechanical Designer, I coordinate 3D CAD models and mechanical drawings. I review routing geometry, support arrangements, mounting interfaces, and installation clearances to identify potential fit-up issues.

### Coordination work

- **Routing and interfaces:** Align enclosure and support details with the cable-bus route and surrounding installation geometry.
- **Clearance checks:** Evaluate space around mounting points, cable-entry areas, and adjacent structures.
- **Support and mounting:** Coordinate support locations and mounting interfaces with the layout and installation requirements.
- **Access:** Consider assembly access, installation sequence, and space for inspection and maintenance.
- **Design updates:** Incorporate changes into CAD models and drawings, and help resolve fabrication and assembly questions.

## Design approach and outcome

I evaluate the cable-bus route, enclosure mounting, support arrangement, and cable-entry clearance as connected interfaces. This helps identify potential conflicts during design coordination and supports a clearer transition from engineering drawings to installation.

The coordinated models and drawings provide a basis for fabrication, installation planning, and resolving mechanical fit-up questions.

**Design principle:** Coordinate the route. Verify the clearance. Design for installation.
