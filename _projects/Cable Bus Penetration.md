## Adjustable Cable-Bus Penetration

As part of custom cable-bus system work, I supported the mechanical design of an adjustable penetration assembly. The work centered on installation requirements, dimensional fit, tolerance relationships, and practical fabrication and assembly considerations.

### Contribution

- Reviewed interface and installation requirements to inform the adjustable mechanical design.
- Considered clearances, tolerances, fabrication, and assembly needs as part of design coordination.
- Supported engineering updates and cross-functional resolution of technical questions.
