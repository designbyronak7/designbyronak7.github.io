## Adjustable cable-bus penetration

## Design problem

The penetration has to pass through an enclosure wall and meet the cable-bus interface while allowing adjustment during installation.

## My role

I reviewed the interface and installation requirements, then checked fit, tolerance relationships, fabrication, and assembly.

**Design decision:** I considered an adjustable wall interface to accommodate fit variation during installation.

**Constraints:** the wall opening, connected equipment, and tolerance relationships govern the fit. Customer details, dimensions, and production drawings are intentionally omitted.

**Outcome:** I supported engineering updates and cross-functional resolution of technical questions. The illustration is simplified and does not show a released design.
