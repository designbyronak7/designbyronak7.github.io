# Junction and transition enclosures

<p class="project-kicker">Power Bus Way · generalized professional example</p>

## Design problem

A junction or transition enclosure has to provide a path for conductors as they enter, branch, or connect to nearby equipment.

## My role

I check opening placement, support points, conductor space, and access to the connections.

**Design decision:** I set the conductor path first, then coordinate the opening and support locations around it.

**Constraints:** adjacent equipment interfaces and the available enclosure space limit the arrangement. Customer geometry, ratings, and dimensions are not shown.

**Outcome:** I support CAD and drawing updates and help answer fit questions during fabrication and assembly. The illustration is a general concept, not a production drawing.
