## Design challenge

The fixture had to hold the knee-crank part during milling while leaving the machining area accessible.

## My role and design choice

I modelled the assembly in SolidWorks, selected the components, and prepared the assembly drawing and parts list. I used a base with clamping elements arranged around the part so the drawing shows how the fixture holds it as an assembly.

**Constraint:** the workpiece needed to stay located while leaving access for the milling operation.

**Outcome:** the college project produced an assembly model, drawing, and parts list.
