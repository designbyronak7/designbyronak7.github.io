## Design brief

This college tool-design assignment called for a fixture that could locate a workpiece for an initial milling operation, then hold it securely at an angle for a second setup.

## What I considered

- Repeatable location between operations
- Clamping that resists movement during machining
- Rigidity and safe access to the workpiece
- Using standard parts where practical to reduce custom machining

I modelled the fixture in SolidWorks and reviewed the concept with classmates and an instructor. The work helped me think through how the workpiece, fixture, and machine envelope have to fit together before a design is ready to use.
