## Rapid PCB-Enclosure Fit Check

### Challenge

The PCB arrived with mounting-hole positions that differed from the original dimensions used for the enclosure. Printing the full case before checking the updated fit would have committed the printer to an estimated eight-hour job.

### Approach

I updated the SolidWorks enclosure to match the received board, then created a simplified, 0.6 mm-thick, three-layer fit-check model focused on the overall fit and mounting locations.

### Outcome

The fit-check model took less than 20 minutes to prepare, allowing the mounting and fit assumptions to be checked before a full print and avoiding more than eight hours of printer time for an unverified design.
